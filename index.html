Services

What We Do

We splice fiber. That's it. We're not a GC, we're not a bore crew, we don't do everything. We do splicing right.

01

Fusion Splicing

Single-mode, multimode, whatever the job calls for. Core-alignment splicers, low-loss results, every splice logged and tested.

02

Aerial Work

We have our own bucket truck. Aerial splice work, mid-span access, lashing, strand work. We own it, we operate it.

03

Underground

Vault and handhole splice work. We're not boring or trenching. We splice what's in the ground and test it clean.

04

OTDR Testing

Full trace on every span. Loss readings, event tables, splice-by-splice documentation. Numbers ISP clients need.

05

Emergency Response

Fiber down at 2 AM? We pick up. Nights, weekends, holidays. We've handled it and we move fast.

About

Background

We came up in-house at an ISP. Built fiber with them for years before we went out on our own. We know what ISPs expect because we used to be on that side.

We started Morrow Fiber Solutions because we kept seeing the same thing: crews that cut corners, skip documentation, or just don't show. We don't do that.

Based in Eldridge, Iowa. We cover Quad Cities and Omaha. If the job makes sense, we'll go where the work is.
