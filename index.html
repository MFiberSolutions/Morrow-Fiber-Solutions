The Story

Why We Started This

We spent years working inside of an ISP. Built fiber infrastructure with them for years before we started Morrow Fiber. That experience means we know what ISPs need and what happens when splice work isn't documented properly, and built poorly.

Most splice crews cut corners. We don't. We own our equipment, run our own crews, and test every splice before we're done. No excuses, no shortcuts.

We're based in Eldridge, Iowa, covering the Quad Cities and Omaha. But if the work makes sense, we'll go where the fiber is.

What We Do

Splice Methods

Here's what we do and why each part matters.

01 / Fusion Splicing

Core Alignment

We splice single-mode and multimode fiber with core-alignment splicers. We measure insertion loss on every burn and document everything.

02 / Aerial Work

Bucket Truck Crews

We own our own bucket trucks, and splice labs. We show up with our equipment and do the work ourselves.

03 / Underground

Vault & Handhole

Vault and handhole splice work. We splice what's in the ground and test it to make sure it passes.

04 / OTDR Testing

Full Documentation

We test every splice with OTDR and hand you over clean results.

05 / Emergency Response

24/7 Available

Fiber goes down in the middle of the night. We answer the phone and we get to the site, and can mobilize in as little as 30 minutes.

06 / Ongoing Subcontract

Long-Term Work

If you need a splice crew on retainer for a buildout or long-term project, we can structure that.
