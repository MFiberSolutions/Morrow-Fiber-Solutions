Services

What We Actually Do

We splice fiber. That is the whole thing. We are not a GC, we are not a bore crew, we are not trying to be everything. We are the team you bring in when fiber needs to get spliced right and documented properly.

01

Fusion Splicing

Single-mode, multimode, whatever the job calls for. Core-alignment splicers, low-loss results, every splice logged and tested before we leave.

02

Aerial Work

We have our own bucket truck. Aerial splice work, mid-span access, lashing, strand work. We do not rent the truck and we do not sub it out.

03

Underground

Vault and handhole splice work. We are not boring or trenching. We splice what is in the ground and make sure it tests clean.

04

OTDR Testing

Full trace on every span. Loss readings, event tables, splice-by-splice documentation. If your ISP client wants to see the numbers, we have them.

05

Emergency Response

Fiber goes down at 2 in the morning, we answer. Nights, weekends, holidays. We have been through it and we move fast.

06

Ongoing Subcontract

If you need a splice crew on retainer for a buildout or a long-term project, we can talk about that. We have done it before and it works.

About

The Short Version

We came up working in-house at an ISP. Built fiber alongside them for years before we went out on our own. So when we say we know what ISPs expect, it is because we used to be on that side of the table.

We started Morrow Fiber Solutions because we kept seeing the same thing: splice crews that cut corners, skip documentation, or just do not show up. That is not us. We own our equipment, we run our own crews, and every span gets tested before we call it done.

We are based in Eldridge, Iowa, covering the Quad Cities and Omaha. But if the job makes sense, we will go where the work is.
